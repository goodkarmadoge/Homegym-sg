About this review

This is an unsolicited external review carried out pro bono. Everything in it was observed from outside the business, as a customer would see it. There was no access to analytics, revenue or stock data, so the findings are ranked by expected impact rather than measured loss. Each document ends with a section setting out exactly what was guessed and what was verified.

Document 03 is a design concept. It is not a live HomeGym.sg page, it cannot take orders, and some prices in it are placeholders clearly marked as such. HomeGym.sg is an independent business and this review is not affiliated with or endorsed by it.
