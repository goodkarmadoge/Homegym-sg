Selected findings

What the site is
costing the business.

Twelve problems were found in total. These are the four that are losing sales today, each observed directly on homegym.sg on 16 August 2026.

SYS.01 Critical

Search answers the most obvious question wrong

The single most natural query a Singaporean customer could type returns one result, and it is not a squat rack. Category pages offer no filters at all.

query "squat rack for small HDB room" result 1 item shows "Vigor Titan G9 All-in-1 Trainer" not a squat rack filters category only
SYS.02 Critical

Google has stopped trusting the prices

Every product page tells Google its own price information expired nine months ago. The breadcrumb trail sends a single step, so the path from homepage to product is invisible to crawlers.

price S$1,499 in stock yes price good until 1 Nov 2025 expired 9 months ago
SYS.03 Critical

Shared links arrive naked

The homepage carries no preview card, so it shares as a bare blue link. In a business that runs entirely on WhatsApp, that is the exact moment it looks least real.

SYS.04 High

The proof stopped in 2021

The newest review on the flagship power rack is nearly five years old, while the footer advertises 4.9 from 1,551. They kept selling. They stopped asking.

SYS.05 High

One number carries everything

Showroom bookings, space planning, product questions and after-sales all land on a single WhatsApp line, in office hours, with a lunch gap. That is the ceiling on revenue.

Scope

This is an unsolicited external review carried out pro bono. Everything in it was observed from outside the business, as a customer would see it, with no access to analytics, revenue or stock data. Findings are ranked by expected impact rather than measured loss, and each document ends with a section setting out exactly what was verified and what was inferred.

Document 03 is a design concept. It is not a live HomeGym.sg page, it cannot take orders, and some prices in it are placeholders clearly marked as such. HomeGym.sg is an independent business and this review is not affiliated with or endorsed by it.
